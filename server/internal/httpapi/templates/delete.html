Delete your Daily Tasks account

Deleting your account permanently removes, from our servers:

  • your account record (sign-in identifier and email address), and
  • all synced task data and task history.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. Encrypted database backups that may still contain your data expire within 30 days. Tasks stored locally on your devices are not touched; uninstall the app to remove them.

You can also delete your account inside the app: Config → Delete account.
